### Focus
High availability, backup strategy, infrastructure recovery, failover design, business continuity.

### Tools
- **Cloud:** AWS
- **Infrastructure:** Terraform
- **Compute:** EC2
- **Storage:** S3, RDS
- **Networking:** Route 53
- **Security & Ops:** IAM, CloudWatch

I designed a comprehensive disaster recovery architecture on AWS to ensure minimal downtime and data loss. This involved multi-region failover strategies, automated backups, and real-time monitoring to maintain high availability for mission-critical applications.
